# Street story: art direction and fallback illustration

`../public/images/street-story.webp` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool as the art-direction reference and loading/WebGL fallback for the continuous street story. It is not a geographic reconstruction. The working street is rendered separately using Three.js.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape website fallback illustration and art-direction reference for Cool Change. Create a sophisticated matte 3D miniature of a Melbourne outer-suburban street on a warm off-white background (#f4f2e9). Elevated three-quarter isometric-like view, one continuous street with low-rise cream houses, muted terracotta and dark green pitched roofs, grey road, pale footpaths, slim green planting verges, simple bus stop with cream bench and green shelter, several sculptural eucalyptus-like trees casting soft broad shadows. Small warm orange accents suggest sun-exposed paving; forest green and sage foliage. Tangible modelmaking with carefully bevelled edges, soft daylight and contact shadows, understated editorial quality. Wide 3:2 composition, entire miniature comfortably inside the image with generous negative space, no text, no labels, no grid paper, no logos, no watermark, no interface.

The generated PNG was encoded as WebP at quality 84 for use in the website.
