The heat we share

Same city.
Different heat.

Across Melbourne, heat is uneven. Look closer and those differences become part of an everyday street.

The road, the roof, the walk to the shops. Start with the places you know.

Room for everyone

You can choose where to stand. Changing a roof, a rental home or a public footpath takes more than one person’s choice.

What does this story show?

These are different measures. This story does not display measured temperatures or predict the cooling of a particular street.

The connected map lets you select a neighbourhood block, inspect heat and canopy, and explore a modelled planting scenario. That map is outside this offline mockup.
